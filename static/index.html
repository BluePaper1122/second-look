Choose samples for a position check.

Choose the samples to investigate and the wells you can spare. The planner places each selected sample and both controls at the edge and in the interior.

Samples for follow-up

Uses four wells per selected sample plus eight control wells. The initial budget of 24 allows up to four selected samples; change it to match your resources. The seed makes the layout reproducible; changing it rearranges wells within the same constraints. No samples are preselected. These are technical replicates; independent biological replication needs a separate design.

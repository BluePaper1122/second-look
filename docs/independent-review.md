# Independent review procedure — not executed

The builder’s review cannot establish an unbiased result. Use a person who did not build the app for the following short, repeatable check. Do not report a result until the person has actually completed it.

## Product task, without coaching

Give the reviewer only the public demo URL and this task: “Use the labeled confounded example to explain what can and cannot be concluded, select two samples for a 24-well repeat, and save a record another researcher can inspect.”

Observe time to first completed inspection, whether they notice the sample-position limitation, whether they understand observed versus proposed counts, whether they can identify the design’s remaining limitations, and whether they save/export without assistance. Record wrong turns and questions verbatim, with permission. Do not treat successful clicking as scientific understanding. Ask what they would change before actually running the assay.

## Comparison task

The official gallery was unpublished at the latest check. The two public repositories in judging-review.md are a convenience sample, not the field. An independent reviewer may compare published descriptions, using the same evidence cutoff and the same four official criteria for every project. Randomize order; initially hide author/project names where practical. Disclose that domain differences and unequal public evidence prevent a controlled comparison.

For each criterion record a score from 1 to 5, confidence, evidence location, and whether the claim is missing, author-reported, or independently reproduced. Do not give our project credit for builder-only tests while treating another project’s untested claim as a proven failure. Separate baseline eligibility from scored quality. Leave unknowns unknown.

## Decision rule

Prioritize observed task failures and missing eligibility evidence before decorative additions. Resolve reviewer disagreement by checking the underlying artifact, not averaging away an unsupported claim. Publish denominators, unknowns and limitations with any score. Do not turn this exercise into a claimed probability of winning or proof that our project is better than the competition.
